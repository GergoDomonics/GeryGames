# Credits

## Runtime services (nothing stored in this repo)

- Karaoke music plays through the official YouTube embedded player (YouTube IFrame Player API); players choose the videos.
- Karaoke lyrics are fetched at runtime from LRCLIB (https://lrclib.net), a free community lyrics database.
